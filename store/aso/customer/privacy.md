# Privacy labels — Customer

## Data collected (declare accurately in stores)

| Data | Linked to identity | Purpose |
|------|--------------------|---------|
| Phone / account | Yes | Auth |
| Precise location | Yes | Delivery, serviceability |
| Purchase history | Yes | Orders |
| Device IDs | Yes | Push, fraud, analytics |
| Crash logs | No | Stability |

## Not collected
- Raw payment card numbers (PSP tokenization only)

Account deletion: in-app Settings → Privacy → Delete account (backed by identity erasure flow).
